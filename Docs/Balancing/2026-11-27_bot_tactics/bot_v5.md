## Rushes

An approach is a soldier closing from over 15 m to within 10 m of the nearest active enemy in 15 s or less, having done at least half the closing itself (being walked up to doesn't count). It is a **rush** if the soldier was moving for 60% of it and seen by the enemy for 50% of it, otherwise a **covered** approach. **Won**: it injured or killed an enemy (rifle, grenade or C4 kill) from the start of the approach to 20 s after arriving, before going down itself. **Lost**: it was injured, stunned or killed first. **Traded**: won, then went down inside the window. Grenade stuns are not credited. 20 of 20 matches have tracks.
